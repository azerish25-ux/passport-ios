# PassportProof Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

PassportProof is an independent photo-measurement app. It is not affiliated with
any government or issuing authority. This policy explains what the app does with
information when you use it.

## Summary

- We do not require an account.
- We do not collect, transmit or sell your photographs.
- We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking.
- Photo analysis happens on your device.
- The optional export purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit.

## Photographs and camera

When you choose a photo or use the camera, the selected image is processed on
your device to measure it against the document rules you selected.

The app holds your selected crop in memory while the app is running. It does not
save that photo to the Photos library, an app database, an app cache or a remote
service. Starting over replaces it; force-quitting the app releases the
in-memory image.

If you export a file, the app creates a temporary file in protected app storage
so the iOS share sheet can deliver it. That staging file is deleted when sharing
finishes or the export screen closes. Any copy you choose to save or send through
the share sheet is controlled by you and the destination you select.

Camera access is requested only when you choose live capture. The system photo
picker gives the app only the image you select. You can change permissions at any
time in iOS Settings.

## Information we collect

PassportProof does not operate a server and does not collect personal data. The
app contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking
SDK. The bundled privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no
tracking.

## Purchases

The optional export unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We
do not receive your payment card or billing details. Apple may process purchase
and entitlement information under Apple's privacy policy. PassportProof reads
only the verified StoreKit entitlement needed to unlock export and restore a
previous purchase.

## External links

Document-detail screens can open the issuing authority's public guidance in your
browser. Those sites have their own privacy practices. PassportProof does not
attach your photograph, session data or identifiers to the link.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose photo tool and is not directed at children. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from anyone because the app does not
collect personal information.

## Data retention and deletion

PassportProof does not retain your photograph outside app memory. To remove
session data immediately, use the app's start-over action or force-quit the app.
Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Apple controls purchase records; use
your Apple Account purchase history for those records.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material
changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store listing before they take
effect.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact published on the PassportProof
App Store product page.
